Platform Disclaimer

Important Information Regarding Service Coordination & Independent Contractor Boundaries in Nigeria

Essential Notice for All Customers & Partners

ALLFIX Services Xpress operates as an independent coordination and artisan matching platform. We provide seamless connections between customers and verified independent technicians. Please review the operational boundaries below before scheduling or authorizing work.

01

Coordination & Matching Role Only

ALLFIX Services Xpress operates as a technology coordination and matching platform connecting end-users with registered independent vendors.

ALLFIX is not an employer, general contractor, direct mechanic, or direct artisan. The physical service agreement and delivery of manual or technical tasks is executed directly between the customer and the matched independent vendor.

ℹ️

Contractual Boundary: ALLFIX facilitates discovery, dispatch coordination, and dispute mediation. Direct technical instructions and on-site access are managed directly between client and vendor.

02

Limits of Vendor Verification

While ALLFIX performs rigorous identity checks, review of government records (e.g. NIN, CAC registration where applicable), practical trade questionnaires, and phone verifications, ALLFIX cannot guarantee that every vendor's past performance is predictive of future work.

Customers retain the right and duty to inspect the vendor's identification upon arrival and assess proposed quotes before authorizing technical work or handing over premises/keys.

🔍

Customer Right: Always inspect the assigned vendor's identity credentials upon arrival before authorizing site access or technical work.

03

Quotations, Spare Parts & Warranties

Any preliminary pricing or estimates discussed on the platform or over initial telephone calls are purely indicative. Final binding quotes are determined on-site following physical diagnostic assessment by the technician.

Warranties on replacement spare parts (e.g. air-conditioning compressors, automotive alternators, copper piping, electrical panels) are subject strictly to the manufacturer's warranty policies and the specific vendor's written warranty guarantee.

📝

On-Site Quote Requirement: Ensure all diagnostic findings, part costs, and labor charges are itemized and mutually agreed before technical repair begins.

04

Limitation of Platform Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, ALLFIX Services Xpress, its directors, officers, employees, and agents shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, punitive, special, or consequential damages resulting from vendor delays, mechanical faults, pre-existing structural decay, or property disputes.

Customers agree to utilize the ALLFIX Dispute Desk for rapid mediation, investigation, and amicable resolution of service delivery disputes.
