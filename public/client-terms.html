Important Notice to All Clients & Customers:

Please read these terms carefully before submitting any service, property, car, or product request on ALLFIX Services Xpress. By checking the mandatory checkbox on our forms, you formally agree to be bound by these Client Terms & Conditions (Version v1.0).

01. ALLFIX Platform Role & Facilitation

ALLFIX acts as a service coordination and matching platform connecting customers with verified vendors, contractors, artisans, and professionals across Nigeria.

Facilitation Boundary: The customer understands that ALLFIX is facilitating the connection and is not automatically the direct provider of every service requested. Contracts and work orders for physical execution of repairs, building, or installations are formed directly between you and the assigned independent vendor.

02. Customer Obligation to Provide Accurate & Complete Information

The customer must provide accurate and complete information when submitting any service, property, car, or product request. This includes:

  • Your authentic full name, active phone numbers (including WhatsApp lines), and genuine email address.
  • Precise geographical location, street address, and identifiable landmarks in Abuja or other service areas.
  • Accurate description of the fault, symptoms, property dimensions, land status, or vehicular issues.
  • Site conditions, access protocols, or security restrictions affecting vendor arrival.

03. Vendor Verification Scope & Limitation of Guarantees

ALLFIX makes reasonable efforts to verify vendors through identity screening, trade vetting, past experience review, and credential checks.

Platform Limitation:

ALLFIX does not guarantee the quality, performance, pricing, availability, or outcome of a vendor's work. Technical challenges, hidden structural defects, or pre-existing material damages are assessed by the customer and vendor upon on-site diagnostic review.

04. Final Pricing & Inspection Quotes

Any preliminary pricing guidelines shown on the ALLFIX platform are estimates. Final pricing and quotes may come directly from the vendor and may depend on inspection or the actual scope of work required after physical examination.

  • The customer is under no obligation to proceed until the vendor's inspection quote is reviewed and agreed upon.
  • Diagnostic or transport fees, where applicable, must be agreed upon before physical dispatch.

05. Multi-Channel Customer Communications

ALLFIX may contact the customer through phone, WhatsApp, SMS, or email regarding their request, dispatch status, quote confirmation, customer satisfaction surveys, and dispute follow-ups.

06. Reasonable Access for Assessment & Execution

The customer must provide reasonable access where access is required to assess or perform the requested work. This includes obtaining gate passes, security clearance, and ensuring safe working surroundings for artisans and technicians.

07. Payment Processing Guidelines

ALLFIX currently does not process customer payments for services unless specifically stated otherwise. Payment for completed artisan work, materials, or diagnostic labor is settled directly with the vendor under the terms agreed upon during quotation, except where an official ALLFIX escrow payment link is expressly generated.

08. Valuables & Sensitive Items Safeguarding

Customers should inform ALLFIX before work begins if valuables, sensitive items, or areas requiring special care are present within the work area.

  • Customers must secure cash, jewelry, confidential papers, and electronics away from the work zone.
  • A responsible adult representative should remain on-site during the execution of all residential or office work.

09. Non-Bypass & Circumvention Restriction

Platform Non-Bypass Covenant:

Customers should not bypass ALLFIX by directly engaging an ALLFIX-connected vendor for future work without informing ALLFIX, particularly where the vendor was introduced through the ALLFIX platform.

Maintaining requests through ALLFIX preserves your warranty mediation rights, safety audit logs, and verified matching protection.

10. Complaints & Dispute Reporting

Any complaints or disputes should be reported to ALLFIX so they can be reviewed. If you experience sub-par workmanship, billing discrepancies, or unprofessional conduct:

  • Immediately contact ALLFIX Help Desk at allfix456@gmail.com or phone / WhatsApp: +234 705 809 9045.
  • ALLFIX will investigate, coordinate with the vendor, and assist in reaching an equitable resolution.

11. Category-Specific Additional Terms

Additional terms may apply to specific services, property transactions, vehicle transactions, or other categories. Specific trade requirements (such as automotive diagnostic protocols, hazardous electrical isolation, heavy equipment haulage, or structural engineering drawings) are incorporated into individual project agreements.

12. Property & Real Estate Sourcing Terms

• Property buyer shall pay a 5% sourcing fee of the total purchase price to the facilitator upon completion of the transaction.

Where ALLFIX or its verified property facilitators assist a customer in locating, inspecting, negotiating, or acquiring verified land, residential buildings, commercial developments, or off-plan properties, the buyer expressly covenants and agrees to the 5% facilitator sourcing fee payable upon transaction closing.
