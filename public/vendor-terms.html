Binding Agreement for Artisans & Service Vendors:

By applying to become a Vendor or clicking "I have read and agree to the ALLFIX Services Xpress Vendor Terms & Conditions", you legally consent to be bound by these Vendor Terms (Version v1.0). Compliance with all 15 clauses is mandatory for active listing.

01. Truthful Information & Genuine Business Details

The vendor must provide truthful and accurate information. Vendors must provide genuine business/service information and valid verification details where requested by ALLFIX.

  • Vendors must provide verifiable legal business names, genuine registered workshop addresses, and active Nigerian telephone numbers.
  • Where operating as an enterprise or corporate contractor, valid Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) registration details must be submitted.
  • Valid national identification (National Identification Number - NIN, International Passport, Voter's Card, or Driver's License) must be furnished upon request.

02. Non-Automatic Approval Policy

Submission of an application does not mean the vendor is automatically approved or verified. ALLFIX conducts background vetting, trade credential checks, past work portfolio inspection, and geographic demand verification. Acceptance into the active verified vendor network is at the sole discretion of ALLFIX.

03. Platform Rights to Verify, Reject, Suspend, or Remove

ALLFIX reserves the right to verify, reject, suspend, or remove a vendor. ALLFIX may audit vendor credentials, inspect work sites, decline unverified applicants, or remove vendors who fail to maintain safety, punctuality, or professional trade benchmarks.

04. Authorized Representation of "Verified" Status

Only vendors approved by ALLFIX should be represented as verified ALLFIX vendors. Vendors whose applications are pending, under probationary review, suspended, or terminated must not misrepresent their status or advertise using ALLFIX marks.

05. Professional and Reliable Service Delivery

Vendors must provide professional and reliable service to customers referred through ALLFIX. Vendors must arrive promptly, maintain respectful customer communication, adhere to technical safety standards, and execute jobs to verified artisan standards.

06. Honest Communication: Pricing, Availability & Limitations

Vendors must communicate honestly about pricing, availability, experience, scope of work, and limitations.

  • Provide clear, honest quotes before commencing work without deceptive on-site markups.
  • Declare honest schedule availability and arrive at the agreed customer inspection window.
  • Decline or disclose immediately if a task exceeds your current technical tooling or licensing scope.

07. Non-Misrepresentation of Qualifications

Vendors must not misrepresent their qualifications or services. Falsifying certifications, apprentice history, trade diplomas, or past work experience is strictly prohibited.

08. Responsible Handling of Customer Information

Vendors must not use customer information obtained through ALLFIX improperly. Customer phone numbers, addresses, and property data must be used strictly for executing the assigned service request and never disclosed to unauthorized third parties or used for unsolicited solicitations.

09. Platform Non-Bypass & Circumvention Prohibition

Platform Integrity Rule:

Vendors should not bypass ALLFIX by taking customers introduced through ALLFIX and deliberately arranging future business outside the ALLFIX system without notifying or complying with ALLFIX's applicable terms.

Bypassing ALLFIX removes service protection records and results in immediate suspension, termination, and assessment for platform damages.

10. Responsible Handling of Customer Property & Personal Information

Vendors must handle customer property and personal information responsibly. Technicians must safeguard premises, avoid damage to adjacent fixtures or vehicles, tidy work areas after completion, and report any accidental damage immediately.

11. Independent Responsibility & Regulatory Compliance

Vendors are responsible for their own work, staff, equipment, licences, and applicable legal/regulatory requirements. Vendors operate as independent contractors and must maintain appropriate safety tools, protective gear, transport, and statutory business compliance under Nigerian law.

12. No Job Volume Guarantee

ALLFIX does not guarantee that a vendor will receive a particular number of jobs. Job allocation is driven by customer demand, geographic location, customer reviews, and response speed.

13. Suspension & Account Termination

ALLFIX may suspend or terminate a vendor's access for misconduct, complaints, false information, poor service, policy violations, or other legitimate reasons.

14. Notification of Significant Changes

Vendors should notify ALLFIX of significant changes to their business, contact details, or services within seven (7) days of any operational relocation or structural modification.

15. Category-Specific Supplementary Rules

Additional terms may apply to specific vendor categories (e.g., auto-mechanics, high-voltage electricians, refrigeration technicians, or structural builders).
