Our 4-Pillar Verification System

Before any technician is matched to your home or car, they pass through rigorous verification.

1

Government Identity

Confirmation of National Identification Number (NIN), voter's card, or international passport.

2

Trade History

Minimum 2+ years documented field work, apprenticeship records, or corporate registration (CAC).

3

Binding Terms

Mandatory acceptance of Vendor Terms v1.0, agreeing to transparent pricing, decorum, and non-bypass rules.

4

Continuous Review

Real customer ratings. Any vendor who dips below 4.5 stars undergoes retraining or removal.
