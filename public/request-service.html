Your Safety Matters to ALLFIX

At ALLFIX Services Xpress, the safety of our clients and service providers is a priority.

Before connecting you with a service provider, we may ask you to verify your identity by submitting a valid means of identification and completing facial verification.

Why do we require this?

This verification process helps us confirm your identity, reduce the risk of impersonation and fraudulent requests, and provide an additional layer of security for the vetted professionals who may visit your home, office or other specified location.

Our goal is to build a trusted platform where clients and service providers can connect with greater confidence.

Your privacy matters too. Your identification and facial verification information will be handled in accordance with our privacy policy and applicable data protection requirements.

Thank you for helping us make ALLFIX a safer platform for everyone.
